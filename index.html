**Rajath Shashidhara** Senior Systems Research Engineer | Systems Research @ Google (SRG) rshashidhara@google.com rajath.shashidhara@gmail.com [CV](cv_1page.pdf) • [GitHub](https://github.com/rajathshashidhara) • [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=47vhbO8AAAAJ&hl=en) • [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rajath-s) I am a Senior Systems Research Engineer in the [Systems Research Group (SRG)](https://techsysinfra.google/research/) at Google. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Washington, advised by [Dr. Simon Peter](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~simpeter/). My doctoral research focused on transport offload---specifically, architecting flexible, energy-efficient [datacenter network stacks](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/bitstreams/d9ff4784-9706-4766-a7ef-dc4aa7a85d82/download) for the terabit era, leveraging emerging programmable in-network accelerators. Earlier in my career, I was as a Senior Research Engineer at Samsung Research and have a background conducting research in theoretical physics. While my technical interests are broad, I am currently focused on the end-to-end design and deployment of tiered memory and networked systems for datacenters.
